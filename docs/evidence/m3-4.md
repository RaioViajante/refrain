# Evidence: M3.4, the definitive public Home

**What this is.** A record of what M3.4 built, how it was checked, and what was not. It describes the branch `feat/m3-4-public-home` (issue #42, pull request #43), started from `main` at `d48711b8fede3031a1e4b6424747f81cd6a39e5a` (M3.3 merged). Every test ran against the **disposable test database**; the owner's development database and development server were not touched, and no backup was made or restored. Everything shown is synthetic: public catalog facts (Grace, Pink Moon, Twin Fantasy, Cleopatra and their artists) with writing labelled `[Test]`, and a generated image standing in for a cover. **Nothing is deployed, the public-reads switch is on only in the tests' own servers, and gates G1 to G8 stay open.**

## Baseline

- `main` and `origin/main` were both `d48711b8fede3031a1e4b6424747f81cd6a39e5a`, and the working tree was clean.
- An owner's `next dev` was running on port 3000 against the development database. It was identified and left alone; no test or screenshot used it. Next.js restarted its worker when `next.config.ts` changed, which is its own behavior.
- The placeholder at `/` said "Development build. The approved homepage is not built yet." and imported nothing. The frame linked Home and showed Albums as the current page on the album journal. The not-found view's "Back to the start" led to the placeholder.
- **The character.** `reference/refrain-home-character.png`: 1536×1024, 8-bit RGBA, not interlaced, 1,949,226 bytes, SHA-256 `ad58315e…ba63c`, byte-identical to `reference/refrain/pages/refrain-character.png` (which the V4.4 pages load) and to `public/artwork/refrain-home-character.png`. It carries a 21,881-byte C2PA chunk (`caBX`). 68.5% of its pixels are fully transparent, none fully opaque (the drawing's darkest alpha is 254), and the corners are clear. It was read, never written; its hash is the same after the work.
- **The V4.4 Home** (`refrainV44-Home.dc.html`, `…HomeMobile`, and their captures) was read for its markup, CSS, and copy: the notebook layout (220px margin, 64px gap), the opening (character 380/300/280px, introduction 23/20.5px, "Most recent" 17px with the title in Kalam 21px), the rows (118px date, Kalam 22px title, 48px frame), the albums (four or two to a row, 40/28 and 30/16 gaps), and the section spacing (76px, then 96px). Its introduction, dates, memory titles, and artist lines are bracketed placeholders.

## Owner decisions (issue #42)

1. **The switch.** ADR 0006 D4, read literally, would make `/` the unknown-address `404` on every default deployment. Asked, the owner chose a Home that always answers `200`, whose listings are empty while reads are off, recorded as amendment A4.
2. **The introduction.** The approved screen leaves it to the author. Asked, the owner chose a neutral interim line, not in the first person and with nothing biographical: "A personal music journal about albums, songs, and the memories attached to them." It is one constant, `HOME_INTRODUCTION`, and also the page's description.
3. **The production copy** of the character was authorized in the M3.4 brief ("If a production copy is needed, create an appropriately optimized derivative"). Serving 1.95 MB as the Home's largest image was the need.

## The Home as built

- **Order:** skip link, navigation (Home, current), the visually hidden `h1` "refrain", the character, the introduction, then one streamed part: "Most recent: <title> · Album journal · <date>" (or "Nothing published yet."), **Recently written** (up to five journals, most recently published first), **Albums** (up to four albums, each by its presented journal, featured first, most recently published first), then the footer's closing line.
- **Headings:** `h1` refrain, `h2` Recently written, `h2` Albums. Cards and rows are links whose names are their text (spaces between their parts, so a screen reader hears "Oct 20, 2026 Grace Album journal · Jeff Buckley"); artwork is decorative inside them; a rating is an image named "My rating: 4.5 out of 5".
- **Dates** are the publication date of the version a reader is shown (the reader's language, else the original, else the other). The date a piece was written is private editing metadata.
- **Not built, as the design system records:** the Artists and Memories sections, the "All …" links, the Albums, Artists, and Memories navigation, and the language selector, because their pages do not exist; memories and artist journals in "Recently written", because they cannot be published yet.
- **Metadata:** the title "refrain", the introduction as the description, a canonical address and `og:url` from the site's own origin (`BETTER_AUTH_URL`, read per request), `og:type` `website`, `og:site_name` `refrain`, `og:locale` `en_US`. No structured data and no Open Graph image: nothing on the page needs them, and a social image of the character would be a new derived use.
- **Sitemap:** the Home first, then the album journals, only while reads are on (unchanged otherwise).

The departures from the approved screen, each with its reason, are in [Design System § Public Home](../design-system.md#public-home-m34).

## The character

The Home serves `public/artwork/refrain-home-character.webp`, made with the approved 404 copy's recipe: `cwebp -q 90 -m 6 -sharp_yuv -alpha_q 100 -exact -metadata none` (libwebp 1.6.0). 312,852 bytes (16% of the original), SHA-256 `e98f523f…deec`, 1536×1024, chunks `VP8X`, `ALPH` (lossless), `VP8 `, no metadata.

Measured by decoding both files (the original's PNG filters undone in a short script, the copy through `dwebp -pam`), composited on white. The same script, run on the approved 404 copy, reproduced that copy's documented 42.4 dB and largest difference of 19, so its method matches the earlier review.

| | Home copy | Approved 404 copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alpha differing from the original | 0 of 1,572,864 pixels | 0 |
| PSNR, all pixels | 46.1 dB | 42.4 dB |
| PSNR, visible pixels | 41.1 dB | 39.5 dB (39.2 as documented) |
| Largest channel difference | 15 of 255 | 19 |
| Channel values within 9 | 99.97% | 99.9% |

A 3× crop of the hair, the record sleeve's hatching, and the mug, original beside copy, shows no visible change (`visual-review/m3-4/contact-sheet-character.png`). The C2PA chunk is not carried into the copy; the original keeps it, and stays byte for byte in `public/` (for the README) and in `reference/`. In the page: `next/image` with `unoptimized` (no `/_next/image` copy), `loading="eager"`, `fetchPriority="high"`, its width and height declared; a browser test holds the request and finds that nothing below the drawing moves when it arrives, and reads its transparency back through a canvas (clear corners, 60 to 75% clear). In forced colors it keeps its own white ground.

## Privacy and the switch

- **Reads.** Both lists start from the predicate fragment and from `servedAlbumJournalAddresses`, the same rule as the sitemap and `resolvePublicSlug`'s "found", so every link answers `200` and never a redirect. The presented journal is one SQL fragment shared with `resolvePresentedJournal`. The read returns slug, album title, credits, year, the journal's rating, the cover's image sources, and a publication date: no id, status, deletion state, count, unpublished language, or writing. A test searches the whole read for every entry, album, and artist id in the database and finds none.
- **The switch (A4).** The page checks it before reading, and the reading entry point checks it again. Off, the answer is the one for a site with nothing published. Five servers of one build (unset, `TRUE`, `1`, `true ` with a space, `true`) answer the same page before and after journals in every state are seeded, compared as a reader receives it (status, every header, visible text, every tag with its attributes, and the metadata), in GET, HEAD, RSC, router prefetch, two segment prefetches, `If-None-Match`, `If-Modified-Since`, `Range`, `Accept: application/json`, and with a cookie; every other method answers the same `405`. A mutation that removed both guards failed four of these tests.
- **Why not byte for byte.** Two requests to one server already differ in a random 22-character key that the framework writes into its Flight data, and a streamed part can arrive at a different place in the document; so responses are compared after masking that key (`masked`, the album tests' helper) and as markup and text. In a browser, the empty Home and the switch-off Home are pixel-identical at 1440 and 390 px (the same SHA-256 for both screenshots).
- **States never listed:** draft, private, deleted, restored (a restore stays private), published then unpublished, a previous address, and an unpublished translation's date. Their titles and addresses are searched for in every response of the HTTP run, with the synthetic private markers.
- **Headers.** `/` is `no-store` with the public CSP (covers from the archive only), `nosniff`, `strict-origin-when-cross-origin`, and `DENY` framing, in every form; no `Age`, `X-Cache`, `CF-Cache-Status`, `X-Vercel-Cache`, or `x-nextjs-cache` on the document. The framework adds `x-nextjs-prerender`, `x-nextjs-postponed`, and `x-nextjs-stale-time: 300`, as on the album journal: the last is the client router's hint for its prefetched shell, which holds no data.
- **The build.** `index.html` and its segments hold only the shell: "Skip to content Home refrain A personal music journal about albums, songs, and the memories attached to them. some songs take you back.", links to `#main` and `/`, the one drawing, and no metadata (it is read per request), no album address, no "Nothing published yet.", and no catalog or `[Test]` text.
- **Transitions,** each seen on the next request: unpublishing, publishing again, deleting, restoring (still absent), publishing again, and changing an address (the new one listed, the old one never).

## Streaming and layout shift

The first build streamed "Most recent" and the lists as two Suspense parts, and the prerendered shell painted the footer under the introduction. When the lists arrived the footer was pushed down: a layout shift of **0.079** at 1440 px in one of three measured loads, and 0 in the other two (it depends on whether a paint falls between the shell and the lists). Two browser tests also failed intermittently, because each part arrives hidden (`<div hidden id="S:n">`, outside the page's frame) and is revealed a moment later, and the tests read the page before that.

Now the line and the lists are **one** streamed part appended below the shell, and the footer is left out of the layout until it is in place (`.public:has(> main.ph):not(:has(.ph-lists)) > .public-footer { display: none }`), so nothing painted moves. The tests wait until every part is revealed. Verified:

- with scripts off, which leaves the page as the shell was painted, there is no footer and no list; a mutation that removed the rule failed this test;
- the measured shift is 0 in 10 of 10 loads at 1440 and 390 px (a web font replacing its fallback can still nudge a line by a score that rounds to 0.0000, so the test allows less than 0.001);
- a throttled connection (Chrome DevTools Protocol) was tried to make the measurement sensitive and was **not**: the stylesheets then arrive after the whole document, so the first paint already holds the lists. That test was removed rather than kept as false assurance;
- the previously intermittent tests passed 48 of 48 repeated runs.

Without JavaScript the streamed part never shows (true of every streamed public page, whose reveal is an inline script), and on the Home the footer is then absent too.

## Responsive and accessible

- **Widths:** 320, 390, 768, 1024, 1101, 1280, 1440, and 2560 px, and 200% text: no sideways scroll, the shell `min(100% − 35.2px, 1320px)`, the drawing centered, the margin column beside the lists above 720 px and over them below, nothing in a card or a row past its box. At 768 px four albums were about 90 px wide and a rating ran into the next card; albums are now two to a row from 721 to 1100 px (recorded in the design system), and a rating line wraps inside its card.
- **Accessibility:** axe (WCAG 2.2 A and AA) with no violation, populated and switched off; every link at least 44px; the skip link first, then focus on `<main>`, then the newest journal, every row, and every card, each with the 2px ink ring; `lang="en"`; reduced motion (no transition); forced colors (the drawing's white ground, real borders on frames and rules, an underline on the current page).
- **Typography:** only Kalam and Alegreya Sans render; the section titles Kalam 700 at 27px; the introduction 23px (20.5px); titles 22px (20px for album cards on a phone); the drawing 380px (300px).

## Hosted-release obligations

Unchanged in kind, extended in place: the Home shows the chosen covers too (in its rows and cards), so the artwork-rights review of [Security § Deployment gate](../security.md#deployment-gate), item 6, covers the Home before any hosted page shows one; G1 to G8 apply to `/` like every public surface, including G2 for the client router's handling of a back navigation to the Home; G8 (catalog wording) now also reaches the Home, whose titles, credits, and years are catalog facts that a catalog edit changes without a confirmation; and the production copy of the character is part of the artwork licence decision of the public-release gate.
